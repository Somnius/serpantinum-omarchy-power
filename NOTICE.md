# Notices and provenance

This is an unofficial, independently authored Omarchy plugin inspired by the visual energy and staged motion of [Ilya Miro's Serpantinum](https://github.com/ilyamiro/serpantinum). iSavant's [CachyOS port](https://github.com/iSavant/Ilyamiro-CachyOS-port) informed platform research. Neither project endorses this plugin.

No Serpantinum or CachyOS-port source code, QML, scripts, sounds, fonts, images, or other assets are distributed here. The upstream Serpantinum snapshot studied by the umbrella project did not include an explicit license; it is used only as a behavioral and visual research reference.
